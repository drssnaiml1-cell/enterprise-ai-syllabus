A message to university leaders

Vice-Chancellors, Deans and Heads of Department

Between now and 2035, every graduate in engineering, science and management will work beside AI systems, and many will build them. Employers no longer ask whether a student has studied AI; they ask whether the student has built, deployed and governed an AI product. Universities that close this gap first will lead in placements, research output, accreditation outcomes and rankings.

This syllabus offers sixteen industry-aligned courses: ten in core AI, five in emerging technologies and a capstone practice course in Forward Deployed Engineering, the fastest-growing AI role of 2026. Every course comes with five units, a hands-on outcome for every unit, current industry tools, an enterprise capstone, recommended certifications and the programming languages that will matter through 2035. It fits NEP 2020's skill-based, outcome-based learning, and its CO–Bloom mapping supports OBE documentation for NBA and NAAC.

I invite you to partner with us to make your institution a model of AI-ready higher education for India. Together we can prepare graduates who do not just use AI, but lead it.
