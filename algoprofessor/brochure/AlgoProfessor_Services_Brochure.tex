%% ===================== 2. AI SECURITY ===============================
\band{Service line 2}{AI Security \& Trustworthy AI}{Secure every model, dataset and agent your business depends on.}

As enterprises roll out models and agents, new risks appear: prompt injection, data leakage, poisoned models and unsafe tool use.
Our AI security practice is grounded in our own research on \textbf{enterprise AI supply-chain assurance}.

\begin{tcbraster}[raster columns=2,raster equal height=rows,raster column skip=8pt,raster row skip=6pt]
\offer{AI Supply-Chain Assurance}{Inventory of models, datasets and agents; provenance and lineage records (AI bill of materials);

\offer{AI Red-Teaming \& Testing}{Structured attacks on LLM applications and agents: jailbreaks, prompt injection, data exfiltration

\offer{Runtime Guardrails}{Input and output filtering, PII redaction, tool-call policies and monitoring for LLM apps and agents.}
